The talk has five parts. First, how these agents actually work, and where an attacker can get in. Second, how the MSB benchmark measures attacks. Third, what we built on top of it. Fourth, the results. And last, how the project evolved, including the mistakes we caught along the way, what we have added so far, and what we propose next.
