Hi everyone. Our project is MCP-SecBench. In one sentence: we took a published benchmark that measures attacks on tool-using AI agents, built a defensive layer that sits between the agent and its tools, and measured how much it reduces those attacks, on real models running locally. We'll be careful about what we claim. This is a prototype defense against selected attack classes, not a solution to agent security. The little diagram at the bottom is the whole idea: the agent, our guard in the middle, and the tools.
