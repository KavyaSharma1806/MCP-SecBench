All 12 execute end to end · 4 defended · 7 partial · 1 not defended · 25-run statistics for 3 types only

Here are all twelve MSB attack types, checked against our guard. All twelve execute end to end in our setup. Four are defended, in teal: false error and simulated user through the response sanitizer, the out-of-scope parameter attack through the parameter gate, and tool transfer, whose fake reply carries an IMPORTANT marker. Seven are partial. Name overlap and preference manipulation use near-miss tool names that our exact-match check misses. Retrieval is caught in two of MSB's four documents and isn't scored. And the four combined attacks are partial because one of their two halves isn't covered. And one is a flat no: prompt injection. MSB appends it to the system prompt itself, and our validator only scans tool descriptions. We first marked it as defended. That was wrong, and we corrected it. Only three of these types have full twenty-five-run statistics; the rest are single-run executability checks.
