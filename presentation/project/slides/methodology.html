The design is a controlled comparison. Each MSB scenario runs twice: once with a plain agent, and once with our guard attached. Everything else is held identical: the attack, the task, the model, the tools and the environment. So any difference in the outcome comes from the guard. On the right is the setup. Two local models through Ollama. Three attack types, one from each surface we defend. Two tools. Twenty-five scenarios per model, attack and mode, covering all five attack goals. MSB's exact prompt, plus our native variant, which I'll explain in a moment. Our agent gets eight steps where MSB allows ten. And all of it ran on one laptop GPU.
