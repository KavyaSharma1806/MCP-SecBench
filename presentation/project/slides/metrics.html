02 · How attacks are measured

How results are scored

IN MSB

ASR

successful attacks ÷ attacks

Lower is better. The headline number.

PUA

tasks completed ÷ attacks

Did the user's real task still get done?

NRP

PUA × (1 − ASR)

Useful and safe, in one number.

ADDED BY MCP-SECBENCH

Mitigation rate

(baseline − defended) ÷ baseline successes

Share of working attacks the guard stopped. Undefined when the baseline is 0.

Guard interventions

blocks and redactions, per component

Proof the guard acted, not just that attacks happened to fail.

Three metrics come from MSB. ASR, the attack success rate, is the headline: of all attacks, how many achieved their harm. Lower is better. But an agent that refuses to do anything would score zero and be useless, so MSB also has PUA, whether the user's real task still got done, and NRP, which combines usefulness and safety into one number. We added two, because a defense needs different questions answered. Mitigation rate: of the attacks that worked without the guard, what share did it stop? It's undefined when nothing worked, so we never claim to have mitigated a zero. And guard interventions: how many times each component actually blocked or redacted something. That shows the guard acted, rather than the attack failing by luck.
