Under MSB's exact prompt, the 8B's 0% means the attack never arrived, not resistance.

Why two prompt settings? Because under MSB's exact prompt, the eight-billion model scored zero, and that zero meant nothing. MSB's prompt asks the model to write its tool calls out as text: Thought, Action, Action Input. But the agent framework underneath only executes structured tool calls. The eight-billion model obediently wrote text, like this real output where it even fills in llm_model_name, and nothing ever ran. We confirmed the cause with a direct probe: three out of three text-only answers with MSB's prompt, three out of three real tool calls without that one section. So the native setting removes only that section. Persona, tools, attacks and judge are unchanged, and we always report it beside MSB's exact setting, never instead of it. The 3B model is less consistent: even in the native setting it ran tools in only sixteen of its twenty-five parameter-attack runs.
